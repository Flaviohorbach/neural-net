2 Teach a model with your webcam

Training an image model from zero needs millions of photos. Instead, we reuse MobileNet, already trained on 1.2 million images, to turn each camera frame into an embedding: a list of 1,280 numbers that describes what is in the picture. A tiny network trained here, in seconds, learns to tell your classes apart from those numbers. This is called transfer learning.

Camera is off. Nothing leaves your browser.

Add at least 10 examples to two classes.
